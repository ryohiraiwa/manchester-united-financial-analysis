# Manchester United | Financial Modelling & Capital Allocation Analysis

**Independent public-data corporate finance project** · Historical FY2024–FY2026 · Forecast FY2027–FY2031 · Model review: 12 October 2026

## Research question

How do Manchester United's operating performance, player-registration accounting, transfer payment timing and debt maturities affect cash generation and financial flexibility?

This project uses three years of reported company accounts and a five-year integrated model with **60 monthly periods**, three operating scenarios and alternative debt-refinancing assumptions. It focuses on financial statements, liquidity and capital allocation—not football scouting or transfer predictions.

## Key findings

| Historical metric | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue (£m) | 661.8 | 666.5 | 677.6 |
| Company-adjusted EBITDA (£m) | 147.7 | 182.8 | 216.4 |
| Operating cash flow (£m) | 85.7 | 72.7 | 178.7 |
| Operating + investing cash flow (£m) | −85.5 | −202.0 | −50.9 |

Figures are presented as reported or derived from FY2025/FY2026 Form 20-F disclosures, with detailed locators in the source-verification workbook. **Adjusted EBITDA is a company-defined non-IFRS measure, not free cash flow.**

The model illustrates why improving operating earnings need not translate into immediately available investment funding. Under its selected assumptions, contractual debt repayment creates funding shortfalls in each scenario. Assuming future debt rollover removes the modelled gap in Base and Upside but not in Downside. **Rollover is uncommitted and modelled funding gaps are not company financing forecasts.**

## Analysis and methodology

- Reported historical accounts and reconciliations
- Segment operating forecast and integrated financial statements
- Capitalised player-registration assets, amortisation and disposals
- Transfer receivables/payables and monthly settlement cash flows
- Debt, interest, maturities, liquidity and refinancing sensitivities
- Illustrative FCFF/DCF and peer-valuation sensitivity work
- Assumption and source classification: **F** verified public fact; **E** market observation/estimate; **A** modelling assumption; **D** derived calculation; **U** unsupported/unverified

## Valuation limitation — important

**The DCF is illustrative, not a fair-value estimate or investment recommendation.** Terminal replacement spending and player-registration amortisation are not supported by a cohort-consistent transition. The reported Base terminal amortisation convention rises from approximately £96.0m in FY2031 to £137.7m at the terminal boundary; the assumptions needed to justify sustainable player reinvestment are not publicly established. Neither the modelled zero-floor equity scenario nor its peer sensitivity is a verified price target.

Regulatory screens are **proxies**, not findings of Premier League/UEFA compliance. Instalment dates, private payroll inputs, future refinancing, taxes and other assumptions remain conditional.

## Validation status

The prior forensic review reported 240 historical input cells matched against company disclosures, 8,406 scenario calculation comparisons and 1,235 additional annual/peer checks. These results establish the scope of reported testing, **not that all financial assumptions are true**. A structural examination of the supplied working workbook found 20 worksheets and no cached error cells or external workbook links.

**Native Microsoft Excel for Mac full recalculation, scenario switching, live sensitivity refresh, save and reopen are not yet confirmed.** The current workbook should not be described as fully desktop-Excel validated until that step passes.

## Primary research and provenance

Historical data is based on Manchester United's FY2025 and FY2026 annual Form 20-F filings and company results. Regulatory definitions reference the official Premier League and UEFA rules. Specific source documents, periods, line items and material unresolved findings are recorded in the source-verification register.

AI tools assisted with research, workbook construction and computational checks. This is an independent educational/public-data analysis. It is not a company-authorised budget, certification of audited accounts, regulatory opinion or investment advice.
