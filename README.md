# Manchester United | Financial Modelling & Capital Allocation Analysis

**Independent corporate financial analysis | FY2024–FY2031**

## Overview

This project will examine how Manchester United's operating performance, player-registration accounting, investment commitments and financing structure affect profitability, cash generation and financial flexibility for the club.

The analysis uses FY2024–FY2026 reported financial data and a five-year integrated financial model, incorporating monthly cash-flow forecasts and three operating scenarios.

## Key areas of analysis

- Historical financial performance and cash generation
- Integrated financial statement forecasting
- Player-registration accounting and transfer payment timing
- Debt maturities, refinancing and liquidity
- Capital allocation and investment sensitivities
- Illustrative FCFF and DCF valuation scenarios

## Principal finding

Improved operating earnings do not necessarily translate into financial flexibility. Transfer payment schedules, recurring wages, debt maturities and refinancing assumptions can create significant differences between accounting profitability and cash availability.

## Limitations

The analysis relies exclusively on public information and explicit modelling assumptions. Private financing, contractual and regulatory inputs are not fully observable. DCF outputs are illustrative sensitivities rather than established fair-value estimates.

The project is independent research, not investment advice or an assessment of Manchester United's actual internal transfer budget.

## Methodology and tools

Microsoft Excel, financial statement analysis, scenario modelling and discounted cash-flow techniques. AI tools assisted with research, model development and quality assurance. Findings, assumptions and remaining limitations are documented in the project files.
